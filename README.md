Vice Atlas — the offline GTA VI trophy guide & tracker: all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies with their official names, descriptions and grades, a cited guide for each (sources named beside every guide), a saved checklist with export/import, filters by kind and grade, a spoiler setting, a six-stage roadmap to the platinum, and a missable watch list. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## What this version is

A recorded rebuild (21 Nov 2026): all 55 guides were rewritten from the cited published guides — PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (20 Nov 2026), DamnModz, GameSpot, The Games Wiki, GTABase, GTA Vibes and games.gg. 54 guides are verified and 1 stays honestly provisional (Bad Luck, I Guess — no published guide has pinned its method down yet); 7 guides were newly confirmed from launch-week play on 20–21 Nov 2026. Every guide cites two or more sources, and the audit strip is filled from the trophy data itself so its counts always match the list — including the corrected 28 hidden trophies (Challenging Tank is hidden; Hot Together and Retail Therapy are not).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the guide works fully offline, with the network off.
