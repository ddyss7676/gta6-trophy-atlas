Missable watch list

Only one trophy is flagged as potentially missable. Every story trophy is automatic and cannot be missed; the watch list below is the whole risk.

How to stay safe

  • Chapter 1, before it ends: meditate at Jason's house. That spot never returns.
  • Keep cheats off during a trophy run — cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx, 20 Nov 2026).
  • Save often. There is no mission replay, so a manual save before a long mission is your only rewind — PowerPyx recommends a save at the start and end of each chapter.
